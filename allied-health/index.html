04 · Our approach

No crazy discounts needed.

Heavy discounts are common in the health world, like $29 treatments or free assessments.

However, these often attract flaky leads that either don't show, or don't come back after they cashed in their coupon. Plus, they can sometimes damage your brand.

Instead of cheap promos, we use social proof and well-crafted messaging to attract your ideal patients who are happy to pay for your expertise. And it works!
